How do I add a new medicine?

Open Current Stock and select Add Medicine. Enter the medicine details, batch number, quantity, reorder level, and dates, then submit the form.

What does “Expiring Soon” mean?

A batch is marked Expiring Soon when its expiry date is within the next 60 days. Expired batches remain visible so they can be handled safely.

How is stock requirement calculated?

Medistock compares current stock and average daily sales against the reorder level, then suggests the quantity needed to reach a practical target level.

Can I undo a sale or return?

Use the matching return or exchange action from Current Stock. Every action is recorded in Sales Analytics for an auditable history.

Should I archive or delete a medicine?

Archive medicines that are discontinued or no longer active. Archived medicines stay in analytics and can be restored. Permanent deletion is only for unused test or duplicate records and is blocked when stock, sales, or return history exists.

Where can I see my transaction history?

Sales Analytics contains sales, customer returns, and supplier returns with filters for medicine, type, amount, and date.
