Everything your pharmacy's inventory needs

Built specifically for the daily reality of running a pharmacy — not a generic spreadsheet.

💊

Real-Time Inventory

Every medicine is tracked batch by batch. The moment a sale happens or stock is returned, your numbers update — no end-of-day manual recount required.

⚠️

Low Stock Alerts

Set a reorder threshold once per medicine, and Medistock watches it for you — surfacing exactly which items need restocking before you run out mid-sale.

⏳

Expiry Tracking

Batches expiring within 60 days are flagged and sorted soonest-first, so expired stock becomes a rare exception instead of a routine write-off.

🤖

AI-Powered Insights

Predictive recommendations on what's likely to run out based on recent demand — so restocking is a decision you make ahead of time, not a scramble after the fact.

↩️

Sales & Returns Tracking

Log sales, customer returns, and supplier returns as separate, auditable events — so your stock numbers and your transaction history always agree with each other.

🔒

Secure Admin Access

Every account is password-protected with industry-standard hashing, and your session stays private to you — built for a single pharmacy's trusted staff.
